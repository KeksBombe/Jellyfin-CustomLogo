Custom Logo

✅ Docker Compatible! This plugin stores logos in the plugin data directory and intercepts logo requests automatically.

How it works: When Jellyfin (or any app) requests a logo, this plugin serves your custom logo instead. No additional configuration needed!

⚠️ Note: After uploading, you may need to clear your browser cache or do a hard refresh (Ctrl+F5) to see the changes. Mobile apps may require a restart.
